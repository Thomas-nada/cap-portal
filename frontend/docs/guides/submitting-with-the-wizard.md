# Submitting a CAP or CIS with the Wizard

The Amendment Wizard is the guided, step-by-step flow for submitting a new CAP or CIS. It walks you through everything needed for a complete, reviewable proposal.

---

## Starting the Wizard

Click **New Proposal** from the Dashboard or Registry. You'll need to be connected with your Cardano wallet — if you aren't, you'll be prompted to connect first.

---

## Step 1: Type, Category, and Title

- Choose **CAP** (Constitutional Amendment Proposal) or **CIS** (Constitutional Issue Statement).
- Pick a **category** — this sets the recommended deliberation period (see [Labels & Workflow](labels-and-workflow) for the full breakdown).
- Give it a clear, descriptive **title**.

---

## Step 2: Select Text (CAP only)

This is the heart of an amendment: you must select the **exact passage** of the Constitution you want to change.

1. Click **Browse Constitution**.
2. Highlight the text you want to amend.
3. Click **+ CAP** in the popup that appears next to your selection.
4. Repeat to add more than one selection if your proposal touches multiple passages.

Each selection can be a **replacement** (you propose new wording for existing text) or an **addition** (you propose new text to insert after a passage).

CIS proposals skip this step — a CIS identifies a problem without proposing specific replacement text.

---

## Step 3: Propose Changes (CAP only)

For each passage you selected, write the exact replacement (or new) text. This becomes the literal amendment text — be precise, since this is what reviewers and voters will evaluate.

---

## Step 4: Explain

Fill in:
- **Summary** — a short abstract of what the proposal does.
- For a **CAP**: **Why is this change needed?** (motivation) and **Analysis & Test** (how the change works, what it affects, and how it can be verified).
- For a **CIS**: **Problem**, optional **Context**, and optional **Impact** — a CIS describes a constitutional problem without necessarily proposing a fix.
- **Links and Files** (optional) — supporting references (CIPs, discussions, external specs).

---

## Submitting

Once submitted, the wizard:
- Creates your proposal with the `consultation` lifecycle label.
- Applies the type label (CAP/CIS) and your chosen category label automatically.
- If your CAP includes at least one revision, automatically generates a **draft constitution** showing your proposed text merged into the current document — viewable from the Constitution page's version dropdown.

Your proposal is now live and open for community discussion. See [Commenting & Discussion](commenting-and-discussing) for how deliberation works.
