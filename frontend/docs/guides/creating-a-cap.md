# Creating a CAP or CIS: Quick Checklist

A condensed, checklist-style reference for creating a proposal. For the full step-by-step walkthrough with field-by-field detail, see [Submitting a CAP with the Wizard](submitting-with-the-wizard).

---

## Before You Begin

- [ ] You have a **Cardano wallet** connected (Eternl, Lace, Vespr, or any CIP-30 extension) — see [Connecting Your Cardano Wallet](connecting-your-wallet).
- [ ] You know which type you're submitting: **CAP** (a specific proposed change) or **CIS** (a problem without a proposed fix yet) — see [Introduction to CAPs and CIS](intro-to-caps-and-cis).

---

## Checklist: Submitting a CAP

1. [ ] Click **New Proposal** → choose **CAP**.
2. [ ] Pick the **category** that best fits (sets your deliberation period — see [Labels & Workflow](labels-and-workflow)).
3. [ ] Write a clear, descriptive **title**.
4. [ ] **Browse Constitution** → highlight the exact passage(s) you want to change → click **+ CAP** for each.
5. [ ] For each selection, write the exact **proposed replacement text**.
6. [ ] Fill in **Summary**, **Why is this change needed?**, and **Analysis & Test** — see [CAP Template Guide](cap-template-guide) for what belongs in each.
7. [ ] Add **Links and Files** if you have supporting references (optional).
8. [ ] Review against [Common Mistakes to Avoid](common-mistakes) before submitting.
9. [ ] Submit. Your proposal enters **consultation** immediately, and a draft constitution showing your changes merged in is generated automatically.

---

## Checklist: Submitting a CIS

1. [ ] Click **New Proposal** → choose **CIS**.
2. [ ] Pick the category that best fits.
3. [ ] Write a clear title.
4. [ ] Fill in **Summary** and **Problem** (required); **Context** and **Impact** are optional.
5. [ ] Submit. A CIS doesn't require selecting constitutional text — it documents a problem for the community to discuss, which may later become a CAP.

---

## After Submitting

- Your proposal is now public and open for discussion — see [Commenting & Discussion](commenting-and-discussing).
- Check back regularly and respond to feedback during your deliberation period — see [The Deliberation Process](deliberation-process).
- When you're ready to advance, use **Signal Ready for Review** in your **Author Controls** panel.
